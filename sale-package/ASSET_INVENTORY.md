## Domain and Brand Assets

The domain may be included in an asset sale only if seller title, registrar control, DNS, renewal, and transfer details are confirmed and placed in the closing schedule.

Brand assets currently include site copy, product naming, platform positioning, selected graphics, and demo content in the repository. Reference materials are not assumed to be brand assets or transferable. Logo, banner, headshot, video, audio, domain, and account provenance remain subject to the owner/counsel review in `ACQUISITION_IP_PROVENANCE_MATRIX.md`.

This public repository is not a complete buyer data room. The controlled/private diligence boundary in `PUBLIC_REPOSITORY_REMEDIATION.md` and `CONTROLLED_DILIGENCE_INDEX.md` should be used before sharing detailed technical, security, IP, or historical materials.

No ongoing founder involvement is required unless separately negotiated.
